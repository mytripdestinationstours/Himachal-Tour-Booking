9 Nights / 10 Days Grand Circuit Detailed Itinerary (Ex. Delhi / Chandigarh / Shimla)

Day 1: Pickup Ex-Delhi / Chandigarh / Shimla → Drive to Narkanda. Overnight stay.

Day 2: Narkanda → Rampur → Sarahan (Bhimakali Temple) → Sangla Valley. Overnight stay.

Day 3: Sangla → Chitkul (India's Last Village) → Kalpa (Kinner Kailash View). Overnight stay.

Day 4: Kalpa → Nako Lake & Monastery → Gue Mummy Village → Tabo. Overnight stay.

Day 5: Tabo Monastery → Dhankar Fort & Monastery → Kaza Village. Overnight stay.

Day 6: Kaza → Key Monastery → Kibber Village & Chicham Bridge. Overnight stay in Kaza.

Day 7: Kaza → Hikkim (World Highest Post Office) → Komic → Langza Buddha Statue. Overnight in Kaza.

Day 8: Kaza → Losar → Kunzum Pass → Chandratal Blue Lake. Camp Stay at Chandratal.

Day 9: Chandratal → Batal → Rohtang / Atal Tunnel → Manali. Overnight stay in Manali.

Day 10: Manali sightseeing → Return drive to Chandigarh / Delhi / Shimla drop.

About Himachal Tour Booking (HTB)

Spiti Valley and high-altitude circuits in Himachal Pradesh are subject to unpredictable mountain weather, landslides, and road conditions. High pass accessibility (such as Kunzum Pass and Rohtang Pass) depends on seasonal clearances by local authorities.
